## Final script (245 words, 64.5 s, 3.88 words/s)
1. A Pearl Harbor pilot was told to crash on an empty island.
2. A hundred and thirty-six people were standing on it.
3. His navy's plan for damaged planes: land on tiny Niihau, and wait for a submarine.
4. So he lands.
5. In a fence.
6. A local man pulls him out, and politely borrows his pistol and his papers.
7. The pilot asks for them back. No luck.
8. But nobody here knows there is a war.
9. So they throw their surprise guest a party.
10. That night, a battery radio delivers the news.
11. The party ends quickly.
12. Now he is a prisoner.
13. And no boat can come: the military has banned them all.
14. Then, five days later, he finds a helper with a shotgun.
15. They burn a house looking for the papers.
16. They burn the plane.
17. The village hides in caves.
18. One sheep rancher does not.
19. He is caught, with his wife, and he jumps the pilot.
20. The pilot shoots him three times.
21. As he told it later, that is when he got mad.
22. He picks the pilot up and throws him into a stone wall.
23. His wife ends the argument with a rock.
24. You see, Niihau was never empty. It was private.
25. One family owned it: no phones, no power, one boat a week.
26. A house with the curtains drawn.
27. The rancher got a medal.
28. The submarine never came.
29. And his plane is still in a Hawaii museum:
30. a souvenir from the island that was supposed to be empty.
