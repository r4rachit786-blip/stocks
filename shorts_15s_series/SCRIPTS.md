# 15-second loop series: final scripts

Each voice is trimmed to exactly 14.3 s (Adam, sped up about 1.3x; the dinosaur script lost one line to fit). A new hook every 2 s, and the last half-line completes with the first line, so the replay sounds like one sentence.

## 1. "30 Seconds" (dinosaurs)
> **Thirty seconds. That's how close dinosaurs came to ruling Earth today.**
> The asteroid hit the one spot full of sulfur-rich rock.
> Thirty seconds later, it lands in deep ocean instead.
> The dinosaurs survive. Mammals stay rat-sized.
> No apes. No humans. No you.
> Because the asteroid was early by… → *"Thirty seconds."*

- **Real basis:** research (Kaiho & Oshima, 2017) argues that only ~13% of Earth's surface held enough sulfur-rich rock to trigger the mass extinction.
- **The "30 seconds" claim:** it comes from a BBC documentary (*The Day the Dinosaurs Died*, 2017). Physicists dispute it, which makes it great comment bait.

## 2. "Pizza Shouldn't Exist"
> **Pizza shouldn't exist.**
> Tomatoes are from America. Italy didn't have them.
> Same with potatoes. Chocolate. Chili.
> No fries. No spicy curry. No Italian food as you know it.
> All of it hinged on one voyage, in 1492.
> If Columbus turns back… your plate is empty.
> So technically… → *"Pizza shouldn't exist."*

- **Real basis:** tomatoes, potatoes, chili peppers and cacao are all native to the Americas and reached Europe and Asia after 1492.

## 3. "You'd Be A Peasant"
> **Without the Black Death, you'd be a peasant.**
> 1348. A third of Europe dies.
> Suddenly, workers are rare. And rare means expensive.
> Peasants demand wages. Lords have to pay.
> No plague? No shortage. No raises. No middle class.
> You'd be farming someone else's land. For free.
> Because… → *"Without the Black Death, you'd be a peasant."*

- **Real basis:** death estimates run from roughly a third to a half of Europe. Historians widely link the resulting labour shortage to higher wages and the decline of serfdom.
