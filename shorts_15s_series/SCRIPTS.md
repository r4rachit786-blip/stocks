# 15-second loop series: alternate history

All three: ~45 words (≈14 s with Adam), a new hook every 2–3 s, and the last line flows straight into the first line so the replay sounds like one sentence.

---

## A. "Europe Speaks Mongolian"
| Time | VO | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **"Europe speaks Mongolian."** | Mongol banners over Notre-Dame, Paris |
| 0:02 | "1241. The Mongols crush every army in their path." | Horde charging |
| 0:04 | "Vienna is next." | Vienna's walls, dust on the horizon |
| 0:05.5 | "In real life, their Khan drinks himself to death… and they turn back." | Khan's feast → empty throne |
| 0:08 | "But what if he skips one drink?" | Freeze + rewind, cup pushed away |
| 0:09.5 | "Vienna falls. Paris falls. No Renaissance. No Columbus." | Animated map: Mongol red floods Europe |
| 0:13 | "One less drink… and today…" → loops to **"Europe speaks Mongolian."** | Back to the opening frame |

**Debate bait:** historians argue whether the Khan's death is really why the Mongols turned back.

## B. "We'd Be On Mars By Now"
| Time | VO | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **"We'd be on Mars by now."** | Roman legionaries planting an eagle on Mars |
| 0:02 | "In the first century, a Greek built a working steam engine." | Hero of Alexandria's spinning steam sphere |
| 0:04.5 | "Rome used it as a toy." | Sphere spinning at a Roman party |
| 0:06 | "But what if Rome put it to work?" | Rewind; the sphere becomes an engine |
| 0:07.5 | "Steam trains by 100 AD. Factories by 300. Rome never falls." | Steam train on an aqueduct → Roman factory skyline |
| 0:11 | "Moon landing? 969. Not 1969." | Roman rocket launch, "969" stamp |
| 0:13 | "One toy becomes an engine… and…" → loops to **"We'd be on Mars by now."** | Back to the opening frame |

**Debate bait:** "Would Rome really have used it?" (slaves were cheaper than machines, a real argument historians make).

|---|---|---|

| 0:04.5 | "In real life, a storm wrecks them." | Ships smashed by a storm |
| 0:06 | "But what if the wind stays calm?" | Rewind; the storm clears to a calm sea |
| 0:07.5 | "London falls. No British Empire. No America as you know it." | Spanish troops in London; animated map turns Spanish |
| 0:11 | "English? A dead language." | Old English book crumbling to dust |
| 0:13 | "One calm night… and…" → loops to **"Shakespeare wrote in Spanish."** | Back to the opening frame |

**Debate bait:** "Was it really the weather, or the English navy?" Both sides argue in the comments.
