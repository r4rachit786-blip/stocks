## Description (paste as is)
The orange left India and picked up a new name in every country it reached: naranga in Sanskrit, narang in Persian, naranj in Arabic, laranja in Portuguese... and in English, "a narange" slowly turned into "an orange". Then the sweet orange most likely came back to India as the orange from Sintra, and Hindi still calls it "santra" today.

What do you call it in your language? 👇
