# Publish kit — "One Vote From The End Of The World" (TikTok + Instagram Reels)

| File | Use for |
|---|---|
| `one-vote-tiktok.mp4` | TikTok (layout fits TikTok's safe zones) |
| `one-vote-reels.mp4` | Instagram Reels / Facebook Reels (layout fits the Reels header and the larger bottom area) |
| `cover.jpg` | Cover for both apps; the title stays readable in the 3:4 and 1:1 grid crops |

Both files: 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 High at about 11 Mbps, AAC 48 kHz, 46 s.

## What's different per app
- **TikTok:** captions at y≈1340. Top labels start below TikTok's "Following / For You" bar. The right-side callouts and cards stay clear of the like/comment/share buttons. The bottom 360 px is left free for your username, caption and sound.
- **Reels:** captions sit higher (y≈1300) because Reels' username, caption and audio area is taller. All top elements start below the ~230 px Reels header.
- **Both:** film grain and scanlines are reduced. Heavy grain turns into blocky artefacts after the apps recompress the video.

---

## TikTok
**Caption**
```
He was ONE vote away from ending the world 😳 #history #coldwar #whatif #alternatehistory #nuclear #historytok #fyp
```
- **Cover:** upload `cover.jpg` (Edit cover → Upload), or pick the frame with the two YES stamps (~2.7 s).
- **Sound:** keep the original audio. If you add a trending sound, set it to 5–10% volume so the voice and effects stay on top.
- **AI-generated content:** ON (AI voice).
- **Pinned comment:** `Would YOU have said yes? 👇`
- **Post:** 6–10 pm in your audience's main time zone. Reply to comments in the first hour.

## Instagram Reels
**Caption**
```
In 1962, 3 men on a Soviet submarine voted on starting nuclear war. 2 said yes.
This is the timeline where the third one did too. ☢️

Would you have said yes? 👇

#history #coldwar #cubanmissilecrisis #whatif #alternatehistory #historyfacts #nuclear #reels
```
- **Cover:** upload `cover.jpg` (Edit cover → Add from camera roll). Check the grid preview; the title sits inside the 3:4 crop.
- **AI label:** ON (Advanced settings → "Add AI label").
- **Share to Facebook:** ON (Reels gets extra reach there).
- **Topics/keywords:** add "History" and "Cold War" when Instagram asks for topics.
