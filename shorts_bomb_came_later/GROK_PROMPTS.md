# "The Bomb Came Later": full Grok build guide
Short, 9:16, about 55 seconds, fast cuts. Every prompt below gives cut timing, camera moves, on-screen details and SFX.

---

## 0. The reworked hook
**VO 0:00:** "No atomic bomb… means World War Three in 1950." → **0:02.5** "Here's how."
**Loop ending 0:52:** "…maybe the scariest history is the one where the bomb comes later." → video restarts on "No atomic bomb…"

Backup hooks:
- "The atomic bomb killed over a hundred thousand people. Not building it… might have killed more."
- "Everyone wishes the bomb was never built. Here's why you shouldn't."

---

## 1. Rules for every Grok clip
1. **Aspect ratio 9:16.** Make every clip about **6 seconds**. Each prompt already holds 3–5 cuts, so the clip comes out fast.
2. **Pacing:** the picture changes every **1–1.5 seconds** and never holds longer than 2 seconds. Use a camera move or a cut on every beat. No static shots.
3. **Sound:** ask Grok for **SFX only, no voice, no music**. The voiceover and music get added in the edit. If Grok's SFX is weak, mute it; I add a real-recording SFX layer anyway (section 4).
4. **No text from Grok.** It garbles words and numbers. All on-screen text (in **[TEXT]** below) gets added in the edit.
5. **Non-graphic:** no bodies, blood or injuries. That keeps it within platform rules and gets past Grok's filters.
6. Generate **2–3 versions** of each clip and keep the best.
7. Paste this **style line** at the end of every prompt:

`STYLE: vertical 9:16, cinematic 1940s historical documentary look, desaturated with warm highlights, heavy film grain, gate weave, dramatic contrast, realistic, fast-paced editing, no voice, no music, sound effects only, no text, no captions, no logos, no watermark.`

For "imagined" what-if clips (marked ⟡), add: `cold teal colour grade, slightly darker, ominous.`
