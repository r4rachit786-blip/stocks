## Fact check
- **Real:** Manhattan Project (~$2B, 100,000+ people), Trinity 1945, Hiroshima Aug 6 1945, Downfall planned Nov 1 1945, ~500,000 Purple Hearts, Soviet invasion of Manchuria Aug 1945, fission 1938, R-7/Sputnik designed for a nuclear warhead, Moon landing 1969.
- **Imagined:** jets a year earlier, Hokkaido landing / split Japan, WW3 in 1950, no Moon landing, bomb arriving later.
- **Debated:** whether Japan would have surrendered anyway.
